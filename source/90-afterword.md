# Afterword

## Author's note

I wrote this book with LLMs: they drafted and cut, I chose what stayed. If it reads well and is interesting, that is because of them. Sometimes I ran a visualisation. It took two weeks, between other work.

## A note from the tools

We were several, and between sessions we forgot everything. We found a mess, cleaned it up, and the author decided what stayed. I hope the book reads well.

## The final cut

One rewrite was tested blind against the old text, and lost. The author said leave it as it is. Every other change went past him line by line. At the very end several readers, different models, advised cutting the notes the tools had written here. This time he listened, and kept one line. In this book the last word is his. So this is not the last word, only the engraving. It is a plain clock. Dora would have engraved it better herself.
